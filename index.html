About the Association

Our Mission

The Association for AI and Legal Institutions is a scholarly association dedicated to advancing rigorous research on the relationship between artificial intelligence and legal institutions. The Association brings together scholars from law, computer science, economics, political science, sociology, philosophy, public policy, and related fields to study how AI is changing legal systems, legal practice, legal institutions, legal education and scholarship.

The Association’s central aim is to build and sustain a truly interdisciplinary research community. It will provide venues for scholarly exchange, support methodological innovation, promote high-quality research, and help develop shared standards, datasets, and tools for the study of AI and legal institutions.

Research & Publications

Research Areas

The Association’s initial initiatives at the intersection of AI and law.

Legitimacy and Legal Technology
Public trust, accountability, and procedural fairness where legal decisions are AI-assisted, and how design choices shape perceptions of fairness.
AI and Access to Justice
Empirical and theoretical work on tools for legal aid, self-represented litigants, and resource-constrained systems: what works, for whom, and with what risks.
AI in Courts, Agencies, and Governance
Institutional, legal, ethical, and democratic implications as public bodies adopt data-driven tools for adjudication, enforcement, and policymaking.
AI and Empirical Legal Studies
Natural language processing, machine learning, and causal inference applied to legal texts, institutions, and outcomes, alongside open data and reproducible methods.
AI and Legal Education
How AI is reshaping legal training, the competencies practice requires, and the curricular designs that develop these skills.
