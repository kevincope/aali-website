About the Association

Our Mission

The Association for AI & Legal Institutions convenes leading law schools, legal nonprofits, and interdisciplinary researchers to investigate the profound implications of artificial intelligence for law, governance, and justice.

We sponsor original research, convene expert working groups, and publish accessible scholarship at the intersection of AI and law — from algorithmic accountability and judicial decision-making to the regulation of emerging technologies.

Our members are committed to ensuring that AI technologies are developed and deployed in ways that respect the rule of law, protect civil liberties, and strengthen democratic institutions.
